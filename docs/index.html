How to read this comparison

The example starts with 100 records and assumes a benefit from labeling. Of 80 complete records, 73 passed the study’s checks. Each contributes one pair per story.

The plain wording leaves outside verification unstated, so “the alert doesn’t say” can be reasonable. The chart counts a specific answer. A comparison that assumes no benefit also shows a 12-point rise.

A follow-up with readers would ask two questions separately. Who supplied the number? Does the alert report an outside test?
